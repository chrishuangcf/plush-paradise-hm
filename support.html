Support

Need a hand with plush paradise? Start with the answers below. If you are still stuck, we are happy to help.

Contact us

Email photodesignch@gmail.com and include your device (for example iPhone 15 or iPad Air), your iOS version, and a short description of what happened. A screenshot helps too. We aim to reply within a few business days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap Insert Token, steer the claw with the joystick, twist it in 15° steps if you like, then press Grab. One token is one drop. A round takes only a few minutes.

What is Claw Assist?

It is an optional helper that shows the toy under the claw and an estimate of your grip. A good grip improves your odds but never guarantees a win.

I ran out of tokens.

Open Settings and start a fresh machine. It restocks the toys and gives you new tokens, and your collection shelf is kept.

My progress disappeared.

Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, resets the shelf, and we are not able to recover it.

Is there anything to buy?

No. Tokens are free, and toy values are fictional.

The game feels slow or looks wrong.

Close other apps, restart the game, and make sure your device is running the latest iOS. If it continues, email us with your device model.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad. The desktop version is upcoming and still being tested.
