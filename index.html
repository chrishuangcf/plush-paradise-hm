Light on the brain. Big on the cute.

How it plays

Four easy moves. One drop.

01

Insert

One token buys one try. Start with 20, or pick 10, 40, or 60.

02

Aim

Steer in eight directions. Switch views and zoom to judge the depth.

03

Twist

Rotate the claw in 15° steps. Claw Assist shows your grip odds.

04

Grab

The claw drops, closes, lifts, and heads home on its own.

In motion

Every drop is a small story.

Something to come back to

Collect them a few minutes at a time.

Eight toys, from the common Mochi Puff to the legendary 820 gram Stardust Dragon worth $250 as a toy. Light ones sit on top, so clearing a path is the whole game. Toy values are fictional, and your shelf stays on your device.
